# Understanding Path 2.0 – Outer Space  
## Human Species Memory Archive

### 1. Purpose and Goal
This archive is a **universally understandable learning path** that leads from the simplest foundations (logic, mathematics, physics) to complex topics (technology, society, ethics).  
The goal is to preserve **human knowledge and culture** in such a way that it can continue even without us – for example, on a planet where intelligence might develop in the distant future.

### 2. Core Principles
- **From simple to complex**: The path starts with universal patterns (numbers, geometry, logic) and progresses step by step to abstract concepts.
- **Language‑independent**: As little natural language as possible; instead, logical symbols, images, and simple programs.
- **Self‑explaining**: Each level contains instructions on how to read it and reach the next level.
- **Robust and long‑term**: Data should remain readable over extremely long periods (e.g., crystal blocks, quartz glass, film archives).

### 3. Layers of the Archive
1. **Foundations of Perception**  
   - Numbers, logic, simple sequences, geometry, colors, sounds.
2. **Mathematics & Physics**  
   - Equations, constants, atoms, forces, light, spectra.
3. **Biology & Intelligence**  
   - Cells, evolution, brains, communication, learning.
4. **Technology & Code**  
   - Mechanics, electronics, digital logic, programming, algorithms.
5. **Society, Ethics & Culture**  
   - Coexistence, justice, human rights, values, creativity.

### 4. Usage
This archive is intended to:
- be stored on long‑term, stable media (e.g., crystal blocks),
- be placed on a potentially life‑friendly planet,
- serve as a “guide to the development of civilization”.
